Trauma is real, and healing is possible

If past experiences still affect your relationships, your body, or your sense of safety, therapy can help you process what has been stuck and rebuild a sense of steadiness.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy provides trauma therapy in San Marcos, CA. Treatment is paced, evidence-informed, and tailored to your readiness so healing can happen without overwhelm.

Signs unresolved trauma may be affecting you

Reactivity that feels out of proportion

You react more strongly than the situation warrants, then feel confused afterward.

Difficulty trusting or feeling safe

You have trouble feeling fully secure in relationships, the body, or daily life.

Hypervigilance or shutdown

You feel constantly "on" or sometimes go numb and disconnected.

Sleep, focus, and energy disruptions

Trauma can disrupt sleep, attention, motivation, and physical regulation.

Repeating patterns

Old patterns show up in current relationships, work, or self-talk.

Self-criticism or shame

Your inner voice can be harsh, blaming, or stuck in old beliefs that no longer fit.

Our trauma-informed approach

Stabilization first

We build internal resources and regulation skills before deeper trauma processing.

EMDR when appropriate

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing helps process stuck memories at a tolerable pace.

Whole-person care

We address mind, body, nervous system, relationships, and meaning - not just symptoms.

What to expect in your first session

Compassionate intake

You will not be asked to share trauma details before you are ready.

Paced, personalized plan

Together we create a treatment path that respects your readiness and goals.

Build safety and capacity

Sessions focus on resources, regulation, and integrating change into daily life.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to talk about my trauma right away?

No. We focus first on safety and stabilization. Trauma processing only begins when you are ready.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth is available across California, in addition to in-person sessions in San Marcos.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.
