When your body stays in survival mode

Anxiety, shutdown, fatigue, tension, and emotional reactivity can all be signs of nervous system dysregulation.

Even when you understand things mentally, your body may still respond as if danger is present.

Body-centered care integrated with psychotherapy

Somatic therapy helps you notice physical cues, increase regulation capacity, and interrupt stress patterns before they escalate.

Treatment can include grounding, breathwork, interoceptive awareness, and other gentle techniques integrated into talk therapy.
