Family relationships shape so much of our wellbeing

Whether you are navigating teen and parent conflict, blended family dynamics, communication breakdown, or major transitions, family therapy provides a structured space to work through it together.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy offers family therapy in San Marcos, CA. We help families understand the patterns at play and create new, healthier ways to relate.

Reasons families come to therapy

Repeated conflict cycles

Arguments escalate the same way, leaving everyone feeling unheard.

Parent and teen disconnection

Communication has become strained, distant, or reactive.

Major life transitions

Divorce, blended families, loss, or relocation create new stress.

Mental health concerns

A child's or teen's anxiety, depression, or trauma is affecting the whole family.

Communication breakdown

Family members talk past each other or avoid difficult topics altogether.

Co-parenting challenges

Aligning on parenting after a separation or in a blended family system.

Our approach

Family-systems lens

We look at patterns and roles in the system, not just individuals.

Practical, balanced support

Each family member feels heard while we focus on the relationship as a whole.

Tailored to your family

Treatment is shaped by your goals, values, and developmental stage.

What to expect in your first session

Family intake

We meet together to learn what is happening and identify shared goals.

Pattern and goal mapping

We clarify the dynamics at play and create a focused, realistic plan.

Skills and integration

Sessions build communication and repair skills that families use day-to-day.

Frequently asked questions

How many family members come to therapy?

It varies. Sometimes the whole family attends; other times specific subgroups (like a parent and teen) work together first.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth is available across California.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.
