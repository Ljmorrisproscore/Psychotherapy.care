Anxiety can run the day - therapy can help

If your mind never seems to slow down, your body feels constantly on edge, or you cannot stop bracing for the next thing, you are not alone.

Anxiety often shows up as racing thoughts, perfectionism, irritability, sleep problems, avoidance, or that constant "on" feeling. The good news is that anxiety responds well to treatment - and you can learn how to work with your nervous system instead of fighting it.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy provides anxiety therapy in San Marcos, CA, with both in-person sessions at our office and secure telehealth across California.

Common signs anxiety is affecting your life

Constant overwhelm

You feel pulled in too many directions, with little capacity to slow down or recover.

Racing thoughts and overthinking

Your mind cycles through worst-case scenarios, replaying conversations, or planning every detail.

Tension and sleep disruption

Anxiety lives in the body as tightness, restlessness, or trouble falling and staying asleep.

Perfectionism and self-criticism

You hold yourself to impossibly high standards and struggle to feel "good enough."

Avoidance and shutdown

You delay decisions or pull away from things that feel emotionally risky.

Irritability and reactivity

Small triggers can produce out-of-proportion reactions or emotional flooding.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. We provide secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth across California in addition to in-person sessions in San Marcos.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.

How long does anxiety therapy take?

Treatment length varies. Many clients see meaningful change within a few months, while complex or layered concerns may benefit from longer-term work.
