Strong relationships need real skills

Conflict, distance, or recurring tension does not mean a relationship is broken. It usually means the patterns between two people need new tools.

Couples therapy at Holistic Healing Psychotherapy is structured, non-blaming, and grounded in evidence-based research. We help you understand the cycle you are stuck in and build healthier ways to connect.

Common reasons couples come to therapy

Recurring arguments

The same conflicts come up again and again, with no clear resolution.

Communication breakdown

One or both partners feel unheard, criticized, or dismissed.

Loss of emotional or physical connection

Distance grows even when you live the same life day after day.

Trust ruptures

Past hurt, betrayal, or broken promises continue to affect the present.

Major life transitions

Career changes, parenting, blended families, or loss create new pressure.

Co-parenting tension

You want to align on parenting decisions and reduce conflict around children.

Our approach to couples therapy

Research-informed methods

We use evidence-based relational strategies, including Gottman-informed approaches.

Skills you can use

Sessions translate into communication and conflict tools you apply between visits.

Compassionate and balanced

What to expect in your first session

Joint intake

Pattern mapping

Tools and repair work

You build practical skills and start to repair specific relational injuries.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer telehealth couples therapy?

Yes. Secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth is available for couples across California.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.

What if only one partner wants therapy?

Couples therapy works best when both partners participate. If only one is ready, individual therapy can still help create change in the relationship.
