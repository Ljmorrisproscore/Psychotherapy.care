Therapy that fits your life

Online therapy is just as effective as in-person care for many concerns - and it removes the friction of commuting, scheduling, and time off work.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy offers secure telehealth across California for anxiety, trauma, couples, family concerns, grief, and life transitions. Sessions are confidential, HIPAA-compliant, and grounded in the same whole-person care we provide in person.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide therapy outside California?

No. Licensure regulations require that we work with clients who are physically located in California during their sessions.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person therapy?

For many concerns, research shows telehealth is equally effective as in-person care, especially when delivered by a licensed clinician using evidence-based approaches.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.
