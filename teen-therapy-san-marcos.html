Adolescence is hard - therapy can help

Teens today face a lot, often more than adults realize. Academic pressure, social dynamics, identity exploration, and family stress can all show up at once.

Teen therapy creates a confidential, nonjudgmental space where adolescents can process big feelings, build coping skills, and feel heard. We meet teens where they are while collaborating thoughtfully with parents on supportive structure at home.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy provides teen therapy in San Marcos, CA. Sessions are available in-person at our office and via secure telehealth across California.

Common reasons teens come to therapy

Anxiety and overwhelm

Worry, perfectionism, panic, social anxiety, or constant pressure.

School and academic stress

Burnout, focus challenges, test anxiety, or motivation struggles.

Mood changes and depression

Low mood, irritability, isolation, or loss of interest in activities.

Identity and self-worth

Questions about identity, self-image, sexuality, or belonging.

Family changes

Divorce, blended families, sibling stress, or parent-teen conflict.

Trauma or difficult experiences

Bullying, loss, medical events, or other distressing experiences.

How we work with teens

Age-appropriate, evidence-based care

We use approaches like CBT, mindfulness, and somatic regulation tailored to adolescents.

Practical coping skills

Teens learn tools they can actually use at school, at home, and with peers.

Collaborative with parents

Parents are partners in the process, with appropriate confidentiality protections for the teen.

What to expect in your first session

Family intake

Building trust with the teen

Skills, growth, and support

Sessions build coping skills, emotional understanding, and confidence over time.

Frequently asked questions

How does confidentiality work for teens?

We protect the teen's privacy in age-appropriate ways while keeping parents informed about overall progress and any safety concerns. Specific limits and expectations are reviewed at the start of treatment.

Do you offer telehealth for teens?

Yes. Secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth is available across California, in addition to in-person sessions in San Marcos.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.

How involved are parents in teen therapy?

Parents play an important role. We typically include parent check-ins and family sessions while preserving an appropriate space for the teen's individual work.
