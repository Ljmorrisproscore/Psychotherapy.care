  title: Common Questions and Answers
  items:
    - question: Do I need counseling/therapy?
      answer: Deciding to start psychotherapy is personal, and having someone in your corner can help you process trauma, major life transitions, relationship or work stress, difficult decisions, and feelings of being overwhelmed, unhappy, or stuck.
    - question: How do I schedule a therapy appointment?
      answer: You can call or text `(760) 454-7249`, send a message through the website, or email `info@psychotherapy.care`; if you leave voicemail, include your name and phone number for a return call.
    - question: What are therapy sessions like?
      answer: Sessions are tailored to each individual, couple, or teen based on your preferences, personality, goals, and comfort level, with a focus on helping you feel safe, heard, and supported.
    - question: Where is your counseling office located?
      answer: "Our office is located at 338 Via Vera Cruz, Suite #260, San Marcos, CA 92078, and includes a waiting room with complimentary refreshments."
    - question: What appointment times are available?
      answer: We offer flexible scheduling, including some early mornings and evenings, with options for consistent weekly/bi-weekly times or adjusted appointments as needed.
    - question: How long are the sessions?
      answer: Session lengths are 50 minutes for individual, teen, and couples therapy, and 110 minutes for therapy intensives.
    - question: Do you offer telehealth counseling?
      answer: Yes, we provide secure HIPAA-compliant telehealth for individuals, teens, and couples; additional consent forms and emergency contact information are required.
    - question: How do I pay for therapy services?
      answer: Payment is due at each session and usually processed by the next day using the card on file (cash or another card can be arranged); we accept cash and major credit cards, and require 24-hour notice for cancellations or a $100 late cancel/no-show fee may apply.
    - question: What are your psychotherapy fees?
      answer: Private pay/out-of-network rates are $165 for individual psychotherapy (50 min), $180 for couples psychotherapy (50 min), and $310 for therapy intensives (110 min).
    - question: Do you take insurance?
      answer: We are currently in the process of being paneled with Blue Shield, Blue Cross, Aetna, and Cigna, and we can provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement.
    - question: What is a Superbill?
      answer: A superbill is a detailed receipt/invoice (including diagnosis and procedure codes) that you submit to your PPO insurance provider to request out-of-network reimbursement.
    - question: How do I check my out-of-network insurance benefits?
      answer: Call the customer service number on your insurance card and ask about out-of-network mental health coverage, deductible requirements, reimbursement percentage, session limits, remaining sessions, and how to submit superbills.
    - question: Are my sessions confidential?
      answer: Yes, confidentiality is central to care, with limited exceptions for safety concerns, abuse reporting obligations, or court orders; information sharing with other providers only occurs if you authorize it via a signed Release of Information (ROI).
