specialties:
  - title: Trauma
    description: |
      Trauma-informed therapy that supports safety, pacing, and emotional regulation while processing difficult experiences.

      Our work begins with stabilization and trust-building so your nervous system has support before deeper processing starts. We focus on helping you recognize triggers, strengthen internal resources, and feel more grounded in daily life.

      Depending on your goals, treatment may include evidence-based approaches such as EMDR, trauma-focused CBT principles, mindfulness, and somatic regulation tools. We use a whole-person lens that honors mind, body, relationships, culture, and lived experience.
    href: /specialties-trauma
  - title: EMDR
    description: |
      A structured, evidence-based modality that helps reduce the intensity of distress linked to painful memories and patterns.

      EMDR supports the brain’s natural capacity to reprocess experiences that still feel stuck in the present. As distress decreases, many clients notice improved emotional regulation, more flexible beliefs, and less reactivity in relationships.

      We use EMDR thoughtfully and collaboratively, with strong preparation, clear consent, and pacing that matches your readiness. This approach helps make treatment both effective and emotionally safe.
    href: /specialties-emdr
  - title: Stress & Anxiety
    description: |
      Practical and compassionate support for calming the nervous system, reducing overwhelm, and building long-term coping tools.

      Anxiety can show up as racing thoughts, perfectionism, tension, sleep disruption, irritability, or feeling constantly "on." Therapy helps you understand your anxiety cycle and interrupt it with practical strategies you can use in real time.

      We combine evidence-based interventions, including CBT-informed techniques, exposure-informed planning when appropriate, and mindfulness-based skills. The goal is to build confidence, increase resilience, and restore a sense of steadiness.
    href: /specialties-stress-anxiety
  - title: Depression
    description: |
      Collaborative care focused on reconnecting with meaning, improving day-to-day functioning, and restoring hope.

      Depression often affects motivation, focus, sleep, appetite, and self-worth. Together, we create an achievable plan that addresses both emotional pain and everyday functioning so progress feels realistic and sustainable.

      Your treatment may include behavioral activation, evidence-based talk therapy, values-focused work, and supportive lifestyle rhythms that strengthen whole-person wellbeing. We track what helps and adjust care to fit your goals.
    href: /specialties-depression
  - title: Grief & Loss
    description: |
      A supportive space to process loss, honor your experience, and move forward at a pace that feels right for you.

      Grief has no single timeline and no "correct" expression. We offer a compassionate space for the full range of responses, including sadness, anger, guilt, numbness, and moments of relief that can feel confusing.

      Therapy can help you carry grief with more support while also reconnecting with daily life, relationships, and meaning. Our approach is gentle, evidence-informed, and centered on honoring the significance of what was lost.
    href: /specialties-grief-loss
  - title: Couples
    description: |
      Relationship-focused therapy that helps partners improve communication, repair trust, and create healthier connection patterns.

      Couples often get stuck in repeated conflict cycles where both people feel unheard or misunderstood. In therapy, we slow those patterns down, increase emotional clarity, and build more effective ways to communicate needs and boundaries.

      Our work is structured and practical while remaining compassionate and non-blaming. We draw from evidence-based relational models and whole-person care so each partner can feel respected, accountable, and supported.
    href: /specialties-couples
  - title: Somatic Therapy
    description: |
      Body-centered approaches that help release stored stress, regulate emotions, and support healing beyond words alone.

      Sometimes insight alone does not fully shift longstanding distress. Somatic therapy helps you notice how stress lives in the body and uses gentle, practical tools to support regulation, safety, and reconnection.

      This may include breathwork, grounding, interoceptive awareness, and movement-based resourcing integrated with talk therapy. We use these methods in a paced, consent-based way so treatment feels steady and empowering.
    href: /specialties-somatic-therapy
  - title: Divorce
    description: |
      Support through the emotional and practical transitions of separation, with care for grief, identity shifts, and next steps.

      Divorce can bring layered stress, including grief, anger, uncertainty, co-parenting concerns, and major identity shifts. Therapy offers a grounded space to process emotions while making thoughtful decisions during transition.

      We focus on emotional stabilization, boundary-setting, communication skills, and rebuilding confidence for the chapter ahead. Our approach is practical, evidence-informed, and attuned to your personal values and wellbeing.
    href: /specialties-divorce
