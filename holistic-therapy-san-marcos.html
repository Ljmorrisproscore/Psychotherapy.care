Therapy that treats the whole person

Symptoms rarely live in just one part of life. Holistic therapy recognizes that thoughts, feelings, body sensations, relationships, and lived experience are all connected - and that real healing addresses the whole system.

Holistic Healing Psychotherapy offers holistic therapy in San Marcos, CA. We blend evidence-based methods with body awareness, mindfulness, nervous system regulation, and a deeply personalized treatment plan.

When holistic therapy may help

Stress that lives in the body

Tension, fatigue, restlessness, or sleep problems alongside emotional stress.

Anxiety or overwhelm

Feeling stuck in worry, perfectionism, or chronic activation.

Past trauma still affecting the present

Old experiences influence reactions, relationships, or self-worth today.

Disconnection from yourself

Numbness, low motivation, or feeling like you have lost your center.

Relationship strain

Patterns at home, work, or in friendships are causing recurring distress.

Major life transitions

Identity, career, family, or grief shifts that need integrated support.

Frequently asked questions

What does "holistic" mean here?

It means we treat the whole person - mind, body, emotions, relationships, and lived experience - not just symptoms in isolation.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth is available across California, in addition to in-person sessions in San Marcos.

Do you take insurance?

We are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We provide Superbills for clients with out-of-network PPO benefits.
