When the past keeps showing up in the present

Trauma can affect emotions, relationships, sleep, concentration, and your ability to feel grounded in daily life.

You might feel stuck in cycles of overwhelm, shutdown, hypervigilance, or self-blame. These patterns are adaptive survival responses, not personal failure.

A trauma-informed approach built around your pace

We combine evidence-based trauma treatment with whole-person care so you can process painful memories without being overwhelmed by them.

Your treatment is personalized and may include stabilization, EMDR, nervous system regulation, and practical tools you can use between sessions.

What this can help with

Emotional overwhelm

Rebuilding trust in yourself
